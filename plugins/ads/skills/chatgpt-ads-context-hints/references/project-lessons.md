# Project lessons and dated examples

Source project: a ChatGPT Ads campaign for an education offer. These are distilled lessons from the actual project research and saved campaign readback, not a fresh generic campaign playbook. Account exports, IDs, subscriber data and private receipts are intentionally excluded from the distributable skill.

## Lessons retained

- September 20, 2026 creative-guidance ledger: use natural hints describing an offering, need or situation; they add context rather than enforce exact keywords or exclusions. The ledger distinguished requirements, platform guidance, product behavior and test hypotheses. Its context-hint recommendations had no guaranteed performance lift.
- September 20 offer audit: context hints operate at group level. The project's per-ad `context_hook` fields explained creative angles locally; they were not extra platform targeting fields. Distinct internal ad names did not create distinct public messages.
- October 5, 2026 five-pillar build: saved summary readback verified actual group membership and all group hints. The project separated proposed values from persisted values and retained exact source copy rather than reconstructing hints from names. A requested five-hint set was a campaign convention, not a universal optimum.
- The project's original context-hints prompt, read October 14, 2026, requests five natural-language hints, useful context beyond ad copy, supported facts, missing-input questions and separate groups when intents differ. The installable skill retains those behaviors and adds source receipts and saved-state verification.

## Original examples from the October 5 saved group set

These phrases were used in the source project's education offer. They illustrate task-based phrasing only. They are not validated winning hints, keyword lists, or claims about another advertiser's product.

**AI Creatives**

1. Creating image ads with AI
2. Generating video ads with AI
3. Creating animated advertisements
4. Turning product photos into ad concepts
5. Using creative references and AI prompts

**Content Creation**

1. Writing hooks for social posts
2. Writing video scripts with AI
3. Editing videos with AI
4. Repurposing long recordings into short clips
5. Adding captions and motion graphics

The source offer taught these distinct tasks; it did not promise a tool that automatically performs all of them. Hints for a software product must be grounded in its own capabilities. The groups shared one educational destination in the historical campaign, so different tasks alone do not require different landing pages or a split.

## Applying the distinction

If the supplied offer only teaches captioning and tighter cuts, "Adding captions to talking-head videos" may be supported while "Automatically generating product videos from photos" is unsupported. Use the actual source facts, even when this yields fewer candidates. Do not copy the example set solely because the business is about AI.

A request to reach a particular country must be handled using geographic targeting in an authorized campaign workflow. A context hint about a product's relevant use case does not enforce that restriction.

## October 14 research reconciliation

Current dedicated writing guidance adds nuance to the original short examples: a product term or landing fact can be reused when it makes a customer need clearer. Neither short phrases nor repeated product terms are prohibited. Existing examples remain exact historical examples, while [research-notes.md](research-notes.md) explains the current documented practice and evidence gaps. No winner or causal performance conclusion was added.
